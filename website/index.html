How it works

Three steps. The first one is a download.

  1. Add the game

    Upload the normal PC download, the same zip you would play on Windows, or drop it into a folder.

  2. Renplay prepares it

    It unpacks the game, shrinks the images and builds a web version, once, in a few minutes. No Ren’Py SDK, and the game’s code never runs on your server.

  3. Play anywhere

    Open Renplay on any screen and press Play. Your saves sync while you read.

Everything included

What you get.

Visual novels from itch.io, Patreon and F95zone arrive as a PC download. Renplay turns that download into a game you can open on any screen, and keeps your library, your saves and the people who share it in order.

Plays what you already own

Games made with Ren’Py 8, and most made with Ren’Py 7, which run on the same engine. HTML games, like Twine stories, play too, with fewer features. Known games come from a tested catalog with their art and settings.

Saves with a history

Saves, autosaves and unlocked galleries sync the moment the game writes them. Every overwrite keeps the version before it, and a sequel finds the first season’s saves, as on desktop. Import saves from the desktop version, or move them with a Ren’Py Sync code.

Phones, offline, a controller

Full screen on iPhone and Android, clear of the notch, with the screen kept awake while you read. Keep a game offline and it plays on a plane, and its saves go up when you land. A controller moves around your library and plays the game.

Everyone in the house

The games are shared, and saves, favourites and play time belong to each person. Sign in with a local account, OpenID Connect or LDAP, or switch person on the family tablet with a PIN.

Adult games, on your terms

Many visual novels are for adults, and Renplay plays them like any other game. Each person chooses a level within the limit you set, and at Blurred no picture of them ever leaves your server.

Mods, dashboards, your own hardware

Edit scripts or upload a mod, and it sits beside the game, so updates never touch it. Prometheus metrics and a Homepage widget show what the server is doing. Everything runs on your own hardware, and the one thing it sends is a small anonymous report once a day, which you can turn off.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

If you can run a Docker container, you can run Renplay. Point it at a folder for games and one for your saves.

  1. Run the command and open http://localhost:8080. Enter the setup code from docker logs renplay and create your account.
  2. Go to Add games and upload a game’s PC download, then press Install and Play. The quick start shows each step.

# compose.yaml
services:
  renplay:
    image: ghcr.io/renplay-org/renplay:latest
    ports: ["8080:8080"]
    volumes:
      - ./games:/games   # games and their downloads
      - ./data:/data     # saves: back this up
    restart: unless-stopped

The image is ghcr.io/renplay-org/renplay, also renplay/renplay on Docker Hub. Use HTTPS outside your home network. Free and open source under the MIT license.

Questions

You might be wondering.

Will my games work?

Almost certainly, if they were made with Ren’Py 8 or 7, which is most visual novels of the last few years. HTML games such as Twine stories play too, with fewer features. The catalog lists the games people have tested.

Where do the games come from?

From their developers, as now: itch.io, Patreon, Steam, F95zone. Renplay takes the normal PC download. Neither Renplay nor its catalog hosts game files.

Do I need a big server?

No. Installing a game takes some CPU for a few minutes, and playing is just serving files, because the game runs in your browser. A small home server or a NAS is plenty. Plan disk space as for the downloads themselves.

Can I go back to desktop Ren’Py?

Yes. Export a game’s saves and they work in the desktop version, and the other way round. Games made with Ren’Py 8.1 or newer can also move saves with a Sync code.

Does it play offline?

Yes, once a game is kept. Keep offline on a game’s page stores it in your browser, so it plays on a plane or a train, and the saves you make wait until you are back.

What about adult games?

They play like any other game, and many people run Renplay for them. Three levels: Off leaves them out, Blurred shows them without their pictures, Shown shows everything. A new install asks once, or you set RENPLAY_ADULT_GAMES, and each person chooses within your limit. The catalog here lists the safe-for-work games, and adult.renplay.org lists all of them.

Is it really free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT license. If it makes your evenings better, you can support it on Ko-fi.

What does it send home?

One small anonymous report a day: counts, versions, and how games from the catalog installed and played, by catalog id. Never people, the names of your own games, files or saves. Turn it off in Settings or with RENPLAY_TELEMETRY=off. The telemetry page lists every field.
