How it works

Your saves follow you.

Play on the laptop, read a little, save. The phone picks up on the same line. Renplay keeps every save on your own server the moment you make it, keeps the version before every overwrite, and holds saves while the connection is down, then sends them when it is back.

Install your own Open the demo full screen

The demo is a copy of Renplay that runs in your browser. Its saves stay in this browser, and nothing can be installed in it.

Three steps. The first one is a download.

  1. Add the game

    Upload the normal PC download, the same zip you would play on Windows, or drop it into a folder.

  2. Renplay prepares it

    It unpacks the game, shrinks the images and builds a web version. Once, in a few minutes. No Ren’Py SDK is needed, and the game’s code never runs on your server.

  3. Play anywhere

    Open Renplay on any device and press Play. Your saves sync while you read.

Everything included

Small touches that add up.

Visual novels from itch.io, Patreon and F95zone arrive as a PC download. Renplay turns that download into a game you can open on any screen, and keeps your library, your saves and the people who share it in order.

What it plays

Games made with Ren’Py 8, and most games made with Ren’Py 7, which run on the same engine. HTML games, like Twine stories, play too, with fewer features.

Known games come from a tested catalog with their art, links and the settings that work.

Saves and history

Saves, autosaves and unlocked galleries sync the moment the game writes them. Every overwrite keeps the previous version, and you can restore it.

Season two finds season one’s saves, as on desktop. Import saves from the desktop version, export them back, or move them with a Ren’Py Sync code.

Phones and offline

Full screen, on iPhone from the home screen and on Android with one tap. It fits your screen, clear of the notch, and follows you when you turn the phone. The screen stays on while you read.

A kept game plays on a plane, and saves go up when you land. A controller works too: the d-pad moves around your library, and the game plays with Ren’Py’s own buttons.

Accounts and sign-in

Everyone shares the games, and saves, favourites and play time belong to each person. Sign in with a local account, OpenID Connect or LDAP.

One tablet, many readers: Who is playing? switches person with a PIN.

Adult games, on your terms

Many visual novels are for adults, and Renplay plays them like any other game. Adult games have three levels. Each person chooses their own, within your limit.

Install

One Docker container, with a folder for games and one for your saves. There are guides for Docker and Compose, Kubernetes, TrueNAS, Unraid, Synology, UGREEN and QNAP.

Mods in the browser

Edit scripts or upload a mod. Your changes survive updates, because they sit beside the game and never inside it.

Dashboards

Prometheus metrics and a Homepage widget show what your server is doing. Read about dashboards in the docs.

Private by design

Everything runs on your own hardware, in nobody else’s cloud. Once a day it sends a small anonymous report you can turn off, and never your games or saves.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

If you can run a Docker container, you can run Renplay. Point it at a folder for games and one for your saves.

  1. Run the command and open http://localhost:8080. Enter the setup code from docker logs renplay and create your account.
  2. Go to Add games and upload a game’s PC download, then press Install and Play. The quick start shows each step.
  • Docker and Compose
  • Kubernetes
  • TrueNAS
  • Unraid
  • Synology

docker run -d --name renplay -p 8080:8080 \
  -v ./games:/games \
  -v ./data:/data \
  ghcr.io/renplay-org/renplay:latest
# compose.yaml
services:
  renplay:
    image: ghcr.io/renplay-org/renplay:latest
    ports: ["8080:8080"]
    volumes:
      - ./games:/games   # games and their downloads
      - ./data:/data     # saves: back this up
    restart: unless-stopped

The image is ghcr.io/renplay-org/renplay, also renplay/renplay on Docker Hub. Use HTTPS outside your home network. Everything runs on your own hardware, in nobody else’s cloud. Once a day it sends a small anonymous report you can turn off, and never your games or saves.

Questions

Plain answers.

Which games work?

Games made with Ren’Py 8, and most games made with Ren’Py 7, which run on the same engine. That covers almost every visual novel of the last few years. HTML games, like Twine stories, play too, with fewer features. The catalog lists games that were tested, and most others work too.

Where do I get the games?

From their developers, as you do today: itch.io, Patreon, F95zone, Steam and so on. Renplay uses the normal PC download. Neither Renplay nor its catalog hosts game files.

Does it need a powerful server?

No. Installing a game uses some CPU for a few minutes, and playing is just serving files. Games run in your browser, so a small home server or NAS is plenty. Plan disk space like for the downloads themselves.

Can I still play on desktop Ren’Py?

Yes. Export a game’s saves and they work in the desktop version, and the other way round. Games made with Ren’Py 8.1 or newer can also move saves with a Sync code.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes, once a game is kept. Keep offline on a game’s page stores it in your browser, so it plays on a plane or a train, and the saves you make wait until you are back.

What about adult games?

They work like any other game, and adult games are a big part of what Renplay is for. There are three levels: Off leaves them out completely, Blurred shows them without their pictures, Shown shows everything. A new install asks once, or you set RENPLAY_ADULT_GAMES. Each person chooses their own level within yours. The catalog at renplay.org lists the safe-for-work games, and adult.renplay.org lists all of them.

Is it really free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT license. If it makes your evenings better, you can support it on Ko-fi.

What does it send?

Once a day, an anonymous report: counts, versions, and how games from the catalog installed and played, by catalog id. Never people, the titles of your own games, files or saves. Turn it off in Settings or with RENPLAY_TELEMETRY=off. The telemetry page lists every field.

What does the demo keep?

Saves you make in the demo stay in your own browser, in the storage of demo.renplay.org. Nobody else sees them, and clearing the site’s data removes them. Nothing can be installed in the demo, and it is not on your other devices.
